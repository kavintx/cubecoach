Getting accurate grades

  • Camera at chest height, 50 to 80 cm away. Keep both hands and the cube in frame the whole time.
  • Watch the hand pads under the video. Each turn should push the fill past the white line.
  • Tracking (top right of the video) should stay above 90%. If it drops, hold the cube a little lower so your knuckles show, tilt the camera down at your hands, and roll up your sleeves.
  • If it says CPU, turn on hardware acceleration in Chrome's settings. A 60 fps webcam counts turns best.
